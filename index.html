Career Aspirations

My career goal is to become a Quality Assurance (QA) Engineer, specialising in software testing and quality control. I was inspired by a workshop delivered as part of the ISYS10401 module that introduced careers in software testing and QA.

As a QA Engineer, I aim to ensure that products meet high-quality standards through automated testing, documentation, and collaboration with developers. This role aligns with my strengths in attention to detail, logical thinking, and interest in ensuring systems function correctly.

Over the next five years, I plan to complete my BSc in Computer Science, gain hands-on experience through internships or placements, and obtain industry-recognised certifications such as ISTQB Foundation Level.

Skills Analysis

I completed the NTU Employability Self-Assessment and reflected on my current skills. The key takeaways from this activity are:

  • Strengths: Communication, teamwork, and time management.
  • Areas for development: Technical skills such as automated testing frameworks, and confidence in leadership.

I also analysed a job advert for a Junior QA Engineer role. The key skills and qualifications required included:

  • Understanding of software development lifecycle
  • Familiarity with testing tools like Selenium or JUnit
  • Clear written and verbal communication
  • Problem-solving skills

I currently meet the communication and problem-solving requirements, but need to improve my technical testing knowledge and familiarity with automation tools.

Reflection on Professional Conduct

Throughout this module, I learned the importance of professionalism in both academic and workplace settings. I demonstrated professionalism by attending all workshops, meeting deadlines, and actively contributing to discussions and group work.

One example was during a peer feedback activity where I gave constructive feedback to another student while also being open to receiving suggestions. This showed respect and a willingness to learn — both key aspects of professional behaviour.

I will continue to demonstrate professionalism by upholding academic integrity, developing ethical judgement, and respecting diversity in teams and workplaces.
